# About Us

Over the past 1-2 decades, we have entered the era of Big Data in Astronomy, which has resulted in a steady and sustained increase in collaborative research efforts between statisticians and astronomers. The result is a new dynamic subdiscipline that is rich in data, sophisticated models, and interdisciplinary activities.

This interest group is designed to strengthen existing collaborations and build new ones, where statisticians are exposed to astronomy as a fruitful area of collaborative methodological development, and astronomers are able to leverage the expertise of statisticians to implement the best models for their science.
